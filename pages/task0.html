Responsive Typography

This page uses custom media queries to keep text comfortable and proportional on mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.

Resize the viewport to see the scale change

Good typography creates a quiet rhythm.

Responsive type is more than making words smaller. Line length, spacing, and hierarchy should work together so a paragraph remains inviting on a narrow phone and confident on a large display.

Three breakpoint ranges adjust the heading and body sizes. The content stays the same; only its visual rhythm responds to the available space.

MobileBelow 768px · compact scale
Tablet768–991px · balanced scale
Desktop992px and above · generous scale
